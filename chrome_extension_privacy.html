Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension ("vMix-Helper Video Downloader Helper") handles your data. We are committed to ensuring your privacy and protecting any information processed while using our software.

1. Information We Access

When you explicitly click on the Extension icon, the Extension temporarily reads the following data from your current browser session:

  • Active Tab URL: To determine the address of the video source you wish to process.
  • Session Cookies: Domain-specific HTTP cookies formatted to allow authentication with video sources.
  • User-Agent String: Basic browser metadata necessary for retrieving media formats.

2. How Data Is Used and Shared

All accessed data is used strictly for the single purpose of facilitating video downloads requested directly by you.

  • Local Transmission Only: The extracted URL, cookie headers, and user-agent are sent exclusively via a local WebSocket connection (ws://localhost:8765) to a helper application running locally on your own computer.
  • No External Servers: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your data to any external, third-party, or remote servers. All data remains completely on your local device.
  • No User Tracking: The Extension does not run in the background to track your browsing habits or collect metrics. It only executes when explicitly activated by clicking the toolbar icon.

3. Data Retention

The Extension itself does not retain or store any personal data, browsing history, or cookie logs. Data resides only in temporary memory during the active execution request to your local client software.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue or contact us via our GitHub repository project page.
